The solution

Two pillars that make the Mittelstand AI-ready

SMEs don't fail on technology, but on implementation. We close that gap with two pillars – one gets AI into operation, the other anchors it for good. Both reinforce each other.

Pillar 1

AI implementer

We truly put AI into operation – as your implementation partner, all from a single source.

  • Assessment – a structured workshop that delivers the most valuable use cases and a concrete plan.
  • Quick wins – automate standard processes (e.g. travel expenses, invoice checks) with the right tools.
  • Deeper on-site implementation – specific processes, integration, guidance to a working solution.
  • Training & funding – upskilling your people and support with suitable funding programs.
Pillar 2

Product & platform

We anchor AI permanently in the company.

  • Secure platform with AI access for everyone – hosted in Germany, with pseudonymization, no data leakage.
  • Bridget as your base assistant – the colleague for management that knows your company context.
  • Modular specialist roles – for strategy, processes, finance and more; the interplay grows with every role.
  • Grows with you – add roles and users as the need arises.

The two pillars work hand in hand: implementation delivers fast results, the platform keeps AI alive in the company for good – and continuously surfaces new opportunities in operation.
